01 · The shift
From parts › pages

Web publishing changed under us.

From assembling pages out of shared parts → to generating unique pages that share the same rules.

The old deal

Reuse everything. Build nothing new.

Implementation partners shipped a library — Hero, Card Grid, Accordion, Tabs — and marketers composed pages from the shelf. Sites got fast by getting boring. It worked.

What broke it

The cost of uniqueness collapsed.

AI tools now generate bespoke, production-quality HTML — scroll-driven motion, cinematic transitions, three-week designs in an hour — from a prompt and a brand surface. Designers know how to drive them.

Now

The library was a workaround.

Every page can be its own page again — provided the brand keeps the rules. The library's job moves up a level: from shape to guardrails.

03 · The bridge: stardust:deploy
Stardust HTML › AEM EDS

Meet stardust:deploy.
From static HTML to a live AEM experience.

stardust:deploy is the open-source converter that turns a Stardust page into an AEM Edge Delivery experience. Each prototype section becomes one independent EDS block: the section's CSS becomes that block's scoped stylesheet, and its text and images become authorable content. Header and footer drop into static repository fragments, and the page ships as a content fragment to AEM.

Generate

stardust:uplift

From a live URL: capture the brand and generate the redesign as deployable, polished static HTML.

Convert

stardust:deploy

Turn each section into an authorable EDS block; per-section CSS becomes block CSS. Content stays editable by authors.

Publish

AEM Edge Delivery

File-based, edge-served. 100/100 Core Web Vitals, Universal Editor wired, AI-friendly markup.

04 · Ship your first page
Two commands · URL to AEM

Two commands. No development in between.

From a live URL to a working AEM Edge Delivery experience. Stardust generates the redesign; stardust:deploy converts it into authorable EDS blocks and ships them to AEM.

  1. Generate the redesign. /stardust:uplift <URL> — Stardust crawls the live site, captures the brand surface, and generates three differentiated variants in one run. Approve the one that fits.
  2. Convert and ship to AEM. /stardust:deploy — stardust:deploy turns the chosen variant into EDS blocks and content pages and pushes them into your AEM Edge Delivery project. Run /stardust:diff to confirm the build matches the prototype, then publish.
